About Us

DreamFibre Crystal University (DFCU) is a large campus in Sonoma County, California, near the location featured in the iconic Windows XP wallpaper. The university is funded by DreamFibre Inc. Established on October 25, 2041, DFCU was the world's first university to adopt the Frutiger Aero architectural style.

The founding of DFCU

In the years following the Great Shift, as communities began to rebuild and technological development accelerated, DreamFibre Inc. established DFCU with the aim of creating a forward-looking university. The campus was planned to bring together higher education, natural landscapes, and contemporary architecture. With its adoption of Frutiger Aero architecture, DFCU became the first university to embrace this innovative design approach.

The campus features glass-fronted buildings, landscaped green spaces, tree-lined paths, and water features. Its design integrates modern learning environments with the surrounding landscape.
